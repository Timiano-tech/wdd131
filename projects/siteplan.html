Website Plan

Site Name

Site Name: Book Swap Club

Why this name:

"Book Swap Club" clearly communicates the purpose of the website, a community dedicated to exchanging books. The name is memorable, easy to spell, and immediately tells visitors what the site is about. It emphasizes community ("club") and the primary action ("swap"), making it perfect for a local book exchange platform.

Site Purpose

Book Swap Club is a local community platform for book enthusiasts to exchange, discover, and share books. The website provides a central hub where members can:

  • View upcoming club meeting times and locations
  • Browse a catalog of books available for swapping
  • Submit books they want to offer for exchange
  • Discover a featured book of the day with dynamic updates

The site promotes reading culture, community engagement, and sustainable book sharing among local members.

Scenarios

Questions your target audience might ask:

  • When and where does the Book Swap Club meet, and how do I join?
  • What books are currently available for swapping, and can I add my own books to the collection?
  • Is there a way to see which book is featured today, and can I learn more about it with just a click?

Color Schema

These colors are inspired by the Book Swap Club logo and reflect the warmth of sharing books.

Primary Color: #C0622A

Used for: Headings, navigation bar, buttons, book covers, and primary accents throughout the site

Secondary Color: #3B2A1A

Used for: Body text, borders, dark accents, and secondary navigation elements

Accent Color: #F0E8DA

Used for: Page backgrounds, light backgrounds for content areas, card backgrounds, and paper-like surfaces

Typography

Headings: Georgia (Serif)

Used for: Main headings (H1, H2) to convey elegance and the classic nature of books

Body Text: Georgia / Arial (Sans-serif)

Used for: Paragraph text, body content, form labels, and navigation for readability on all devices

Accent Font: Georgia Italic (optional)

Used for: Book titles, featured book descriptions, and special callouts
