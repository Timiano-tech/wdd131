Community Porch Exchange

Monday-Friday 2:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.

Offer a book any weekday and leave it at the porch drop-off for neighbors to collect.

How to participate

  • Choose a book from your shelf or from the current book list.
  • Bring it to an event or offer it online through the form page.
  • Pick a new read from the community table or request one from another member.
