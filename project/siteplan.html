Local Book Swap Club - Website Plan

Site Name

Site Name: Book Swap Club

Why this name:

"Book Swap Club" clearly communicates the purpose of the website, a community dedicated to exchanging books. The name is memorable, easy to spell, and immediately tells visitors what the site is about. It emphasizes community ("club") and the primary action ("swap"), making it perfect for a local book exchange platform.

Site Purpose

Book Swap Club is a local community platform for book enthusiasts to exchange, discover, and share books. The website provides a central hub where members can:

  • View upcoming club meeting times and locations
  • Browse a catalog of books available for swapping
  • Submit books they want to offer for exchange
  • Discover a featured book of the day with dynamic updates

The site promotes reading culture, community engagement, and sustainable book sharing among local members.

Scenarios

Questions your target audience might ask:

  • When and where does the Book Swap Club meet, and how do I join?
  • What books are currently available for swapping, and can I add my own books to the collection?
  • Is there a way to see which book is featured today, and can I learn more about it with just a click?

Typography

Font Strategy: The site uses a combination of elegant serif and clean sans-serif fonts to balance classic book culture with modern web readability.

Font 1: Georgia (Serif)

Used for: Main page headings (H1, H2, H3), section titles, and featured book titles

Example: "Book Swap Club" | "Today's Featured Book" | "Upcoming Meetings"

Why: Georgia conveys elegance, tradition, and the literary nature of books while remaining highly readable on screens.

Font 2: Segoe UI / Arial (Sans-serif)

Used for: Body paragraphs, form labels, buttons, navigation menus, and general body copy

Example: "Submit a book for swapping. Tell us the title, author, and condition. Our community will love your contribution!"

Why: Clean, modern sans-serif ensures excellent readability on all devices (mobile, tablet, desktop) and creates a friendly, approachable feel.

Font 3: Georgia Bold (Serif) - Optional Special Use

Used for: Book author names, important callouts, emphasis text, and quote attributions

Example: Author: J.R.R. Tolkien | "A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies." - George R.R. Martin

Why: Bold Georgia provides visual hierarchy and draws attention to important book information while maintaining design consistency.
